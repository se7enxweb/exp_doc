Exponential Basic is the small CMS of the 2.x line, "for everyone, educational purposes, personal use" in its own description. It runs on PHP 8.2 or later with MySQL, PostgreSQL, SQLite or Informix. It is a code base of its own, not a version of Exponential 6, and has its own website, basic.exponential.earth.

Repository: se7enxweb/exponentialbasic. Package: se7enxweb/exponentialbasic on Packagist.
