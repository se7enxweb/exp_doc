Exponential Platform Legacy is a Symfony platform with a full Exponential 6 inside: two kernels sharing one database, so the Exponential administration and its templates run next to the platform's Symfony administration. It is for existing Exponential sites that want Symfony next to them and move forward step by step, and for teams that need both stacks at once.

There is one line per Symfony platform generation, each with its own branch and tags: 2.5 (eZ Platform 2.5, Symfony 3.4), 3.3 (eZ Platform 3.3, Symfony 5.4), 4.6 (Ibexa Open Source 4.6, Symfony 5) and 5.0 (Platform v5, Symfony 7.4). The 4.6 line requires Exponential Layouts; on the 5.0 line it is added on PHP 8.4 and later.
