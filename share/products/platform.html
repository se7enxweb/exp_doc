Exponential Platform is the Symfony based family of Exponential: the free continuation of eZ Platform and Ibexa Open Source, maintained by 7x for current PHP. It comes in three distributions: Legacy (a Symfony platform with the Exponential 6 kernel inside), Nexus (a finished website with Exponential Layouts and demo content) and DXP (Symfony only).

Below are the spaces of the former documentation center of the 5.x era, kept as they were: the Exponential 5.x and 5.1 manuals under Legacy, the eZ Platform developer, technical and user documentation under DXP, and the other spaces here.

All distributions at a glance: github.com/se7enxweb, and the help center's guide "Distributions of Exponential".
