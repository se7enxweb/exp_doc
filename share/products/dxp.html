Exponential Platform DXP is a Symfony-only digital experience platform: the free continuation of the eZ Platform and Ibexa Open Source line, maintained by 7x. It is for new Symfony projects, and for teams leaving eZ Platform or Ibexa Open Source who want current PHP.

Platform v5 (the default branch) runs on Symfony 7.4 LTS and PHP 8.3 or later, with the admin UI, REST, GraphQL, the HTTP cache, Solr and core search; the 4.6 branch runs on Symfony 5 and PHP 7.4 to 8.5. It ships no layouts editor and no Exponential 6 kernel; for Exponential 6 next to Symfony choose Legacy or Nexus.

Repositories: the skeleton se7enxweb/exponential-platform-dxp-skeleton (the project you create) and the metapackage se7enxweb/exponential-platform-dxp. Packages: se7enxweb/exponential-platform-dxp-skeleton and se7enxweb/exponential-platform-dxp on Packagist.
