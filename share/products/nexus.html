Exponential Platform Nexus is a finished website project: the Netgen media site (its site bundle, designs and demo content), Exponential Layouts, Tags and the Netgen site API on the Symfony platform, with SQLite as the zero-configuration default database. It is for agencies and teams that want a complete website with demo content, a page builder and an administration in minutes, and then change it into their own.

7x versions Nexus in four lines. The current line is 1.3.0.x (newest release 1.3.0.11): Nexus v5 on Exponential Platform DXP 5, Symfony 7.4 LTS, PHP 8.4 or later, Exponential Layouts 2 and SQLite by default. The earlier lines are 1.2.0.x (v1.2.0.5; Ibexa Open Source 4.6, Symfony 5.4, PHP 8.2 or later), 1.1.0.x (v1.1.0.14; eZ Platform 3.3 with Exponential 6 inside, Symfony 5.4, PHP 8.0 or later) and 1.0.0.x (1.0.0.11; eZ Platform 2.5 with Exponential 6 inside, Symfony 3.4). The v2.5.0.x tags on the default branch follow the upstream numbering and are not a Nexus line. With composer create-project, always give a version, for example se7enxweb/exponential-platform-nexus:1.3.0.11: without one, Composer installs the upstream Netgen media site, which Packagist lists under the same name.
