Exponential Platform Nexus is a finished website project: the Symfony platform with the Netgen media site (its site bundle, designs and demo content), Exponential Layouts, Tags and the Netgen site API, and on the older lines Exponential 6 next to it. It is for agencies and teams that want a complete website with demo content, a page builder and an administration in minutes, and then change it into their own.

Lines: 2.5 and 1.0.0.x (eZ Platform 2.5, Symfony 3.4), 1.1.0.x (eZ Platform 3.3, Symfony 5.4), 1.2.0.x (Ibexa Open Source 4.6) and 1.3.0.x (Platform v5, Symfony 7.4, PHP 8.4 or later). With composer create-project, always give a version: without one, Composer installs the upstream Netgen media site, which Packagist lists under the same name.

Repository: se7enxweb/exponential-platform-nexus. Package: se7enxweb/exponential-platform-nexus on Packagist.
